ANIMATIED FILMS

This section is not fully done and still in working procress, but here are my Favorite Animated Features from every major studio:

DISNEY ANIMATION STUDIOS (GCI ERA 2005-NOW)

I will never forget when I saw this film 9 years ago when I was in 5th Grade. As a kid, I was fascinated that Nick Wilde has the same name as me (when I was referred as Nick at the time). I love how this film isn't sugar coded. There are some elements that, to some degree, feel like a PG-13 film like Young Judy getting scratched by Gideon Gray, going into political territory, and Young Nick getting muscled by a Boy Scout Group. I'll admit, when it comes to this film when I was a child, I never cared much about the story. As a child, the things I like about Zootopia are the characters (Nick Wilde is my personal favorite) and the animation. But as time goes by, I gratefully appreciate how this story is written and the theme that it's going for at the time along the ground elements springled in it. This is my personal favorite animated feature of the 2010 decade.

My runner-up would be Zootopia 2. I had a hard time choosing between the first and second film of Zootopia. As of right now, I am preferring the original a bit more. 3rd Place goes to the Wreck-It Ralph. Honorable Mentions would go to Big Hero 6, Encanto, and Meet The Robinsons.

*Considering that I was born in 2004, I mainly grew up watching Disney animated films from 2005 to now expect from Pinocchio (1940). So, to make it fair, I decided to split the era's up where I dubbed them the 2D ERA (1940-2004) and the GCI ERA (2005-NOW). The 2D ERA contains films that are in a 2D animation style while the GCI ERA contains films that are in a 3D GCI animation style and it would also include The Princess and The Frog (2009) and Winnie The Pooh (2011) since those films were released in the CGI era, even though both are 2D animation.

DREAMWORKS ANIMATION STUDIOS

I will admit, when I heard and saw the first trailer of The Wild Robot, I was not that interested in the film. Around the time when I saw Despicable Me 4 with a friend, one of the new trailers for The Wild Robot came out and that's were my interest in the film kicked in. I like the animation that it is presently - clearly similar to Puss In Boots The Last Wish, Bright Bill is cute, and the line said from Roz "We must become more than what're programmed to me" stood out to me. When it finally came out to theaters, I saw it and wow... I did not expect the film to be this mind blowing. It blew me away. The soundtrack and emotions were the highlights of the film. I especially adore the scene where Bright Bill was ready to leave along with the song I Could Use A Boost playing, oh my god... I always tear up when I watch this scene. As of right now, this is my personal favorite animated feature of the 2020 decade.

My runner-up would be Puss In Boots The Last Wish. Similar to Zootopia 1 & 2, I had a difficult time choosing between Puss In Boots The Last Wish or The Wild Robot. Puss In Boots The Last Wish is almost close to the edge of The Wild Robot, but The Wild Robot has a score that I enjoy listening to a lot and more emotions. 3rd Place goes to the How To Train Your Dragon series (all films are great, hard to choose one). Honorable Mentions would go to The Bad Guys films, Kung Fu Panda 1 & 2, Captain Underpants, and Mr. Peabody & Sherman

PIXAR ANIMATION STUDIOS

I am a huge PIXAR fan. There are a lot of PIXAR films that I love, but if I have to choose one, I would pick Inside Out. I seriously love this. It easy to relate to the characters and I like how the message connects with my own philosophy on emotions. I don't care who you are, there is no problem with embracing all of your emotions. While it is good that you put on a joyful mood, it is one hundred percent okay to feel scared, disgusted, angry, or miserable and have a moment to cry. I like that this film didn't have the message being like "you should always be happy because that's how to live a good life". I would highly recommend people studying psychology or related field to watch this film. This film is my runner-up for personal favorite animated feature of the 2010 decade and the reason why I serious got into PIXAR.

My runners-ups would be Inside Out 2 and Soul. 3rd Place goes to The Incredibles. Honorable Mentions would go to the Toy Story series and Coco
